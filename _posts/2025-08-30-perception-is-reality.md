## Example: Conveying feedback...

Conveying good feedback is essential for leads.
However, a lot leads do make mistakes when conveying feedback.
They often see their perception as the only truth, but this is not right.
There are a lot of guides telling you to start feedback with sentences like "I have the feeling" or "I believe" and so on.

## Example: Buying the wrong type of cars

Before 2025, my former company set up processes to buy each car from customers.
It does not matter whether we could sell the car or not.
Much simplified, we perceived that we do great.
But this was wrong from diverse angles: 1) Strategy to buy every car was not aligned, 2) There was a believe that specific makes and models always work, 3) There was a wrong measure/metric about the revenue per car.
